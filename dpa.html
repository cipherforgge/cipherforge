Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Jurisdiction: New York, USA • Version 1.8
Model Agreement Notice: This Data Processing Agreement serves as a benchmark standard for enterprise cybersecurity assessments. It outlines controller-to-processor obligations under GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679) and CCPA/CPRA. For personalized enterprise schedules, contact legal@cipherforge.io.

1. Scope, Roles, and Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) supplements the Master Services Agreement between Client (“Data Controller”) and Cipherforge LLC (“Data Processor”).

The scope of processing is strictly limited to the execution of authorized cybersecurity audits, penetration tests, code assessments, red team adversary simulations, and digital forensic investigations requested by Client.

2. Categories of Processed Data

In performing offensive and advisory security services, Cipherforge may temporarily handle:

  • Client Personnel Information: Names, corporate emails, system usernames, and communication logs.
  • System Telemetry & Network Artifacts: IP addresses, network packet traces, server configuration logs, and application execution logs.
  • Vulnerability Evidence: Proof-of-concept exploit artifacts, memory dumps, and database schemas obtained under authorized assessment scopes.

Cipherforge will never extract or store bulk unencrypted consumer financial or healthcare records beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate reproducible vulnerability impact.

3. Technical and Organizational Security Controls

Cipherforge maintains robust technical safeguards, including:

  • Cryptographic Controls: All engagement data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 with hardware-security-module (HSM) managed keys.
  • Strict Access Control: Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA) via FIDO2 hardware tokens, and least-privilege compartmentalization.
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Security event logging capturing all researcher interactions with client staging and testing environments.
  • Air-Gapped Analysis: Malware triage and exploit payload compilation occur within strictly segregated virtual execution sandboxes.

4. Authorized Subprocessors

Client grants general written authorization for Cipherforge to engage subprocessors strictly for underlying infrastructure support (e.g., enterprise cloud compute providers and encrypted email routing).

Cipherforge imposes contractual data protection obligations on all subprocessors that are no less protective than those set forth in this DPA. Any proposed change to subprocessors will be notified to Client at least 15 days in advance.

5. Security Incident and Breach Notification (48h SLA)

In the event Cipherforge confirms a security incident involving unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of Client Personal Data, Cipherforge shall notify Client in writing without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of confirmation.

The notification shall outline the nature of the breach, affected data categories, estimated scope, and immediate mitigation actions taken.

6. Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Cipherforge shall provide reasonable assistance to Client in fulfilling its obligations to respond to data subjects exercising their statutory rights under GDPR, CCPA, or applicable data protection laws (including rights of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability).

7. International Data Transfers (SCCs)

Where processing involves cross-border transfers of Personal Data from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to jurisdictions without an adequacy decision, the parties agree to incorporate and adhere to the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor).

8. Deletion and Return of Personal Data

Upon completion of the cybersecurity engagement, Cipherforge shall securely return or cryptographically delete all Client Personal Data in its possession within thirty (30) days, in accordance with the Cipherforge Data Handling Policy, unless retention is mandated by applicable statutory law.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, with exclusive venue in the courts of New York County, except where mandatory provisions of applicable data protection laws require otherwise.
