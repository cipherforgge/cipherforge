Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Jurisdiction: New York, USA • Version 2.4
Model Agreement Notice: This document represents Cipherforge’s standard bilateral non-disclosure agreement provided for technical transparency and reference. It does not constitute formal legal counsel. For bilateral execution with your legal department, contact legal@cipherforge.io.

1. Parties and Intent

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Cipherforge LLC, a New York limited liability company having its primary place of business at 245 W 29th St, Suite 12B, New York, NY 10001 (“Cipherforge”), and the corporate client or prospective client entity (“Client”).

The parties intend to engage in discussions and technical activities regarding authorized offensive security assessments, penetration testing, red teaming, vulnerability research, and digital forensics (“Authorized Purpose”). In connection with this Authorized Purpose, either party may disclose proprietary, confidential, or security-sensitive information to the other.

2. Definition of Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means any proprietary information, technical data, trade secrets, or know-how disclosed by either party (“Disclosing Party”) to the other (“Receiving Party”), whether oral or written, including but not limited to:

  • Security Findings and Telemetry: Unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities, proof-of-concept exploits, penetration test reports, attack graphs, network topology maps, and forensic memory captures.
  • Client Assets: Source code repositories, staging/production credentials, API keys, internal architecture designs, customer personally identifiable information (PII), and intellectual property.
  • Commercial & Strategic Data: Scope documents, engagement pricing, timelines, business operations, and methodology toolsets.

Information disclosed orally shall be treated as Confidential Information if identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or if its nature would reasonably indicate confidentiality to a technical practitioner.

3. Exclusions from Confidential Treatment

Confidential Information does not include information that:

  • Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Receiving Party;
  • Was already in the lawful possession of Receiving Party prior to disclosure without confidentiality restrictions;
  • Is independently developed by Receiving Party’s personnel without reference to or reliance upon Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information;
  • Is rightfully received from a third party without an obligation of confidentiality; or
  • Is required to be disclosed by applicable statute, regulatory order, or valid subpoena issued by a court of competent jurisdiction, provided Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to Disclosing Party prior to disclosure.

4. Obligations of Receiving Party

Each party agrees to:

  • Use Confidential Information solely for the Authorized Purpose defined herein;
  • Exercise at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own sensitive trade secrets, and in no event less than reasonable care, including AES-256 encrypted storage and role-based access restrictions;
  • Restrict access to Confidential Information solely to employees, research fellows, and legal advisors who have a direct need-to-know and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement;
  • Refrain from reverse engineering, decompiling, or publishing any finding or vulnerability without explicit prior written authorization from Disclosing Party.

5. Term and Duration

This Agreement governs disclosures made from the Effective Date. The duty to protect Confidential Information survives for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure, except for trade secrets and critical unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities, which shall remain protected in perpetuity until lawful public disclosure by Disclosing Party.

6. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon written request of Disclosing Party, or upon conclusion of the security audit engagement, Receiving Party shall promptly delete, shred, or cryptographically purge all tangible materials containing Confidential Information, including local caches, memory dumps, and interim scan outputs. A senior technical officer shall certify compliance in writing upon request, subject to standard archival and compliance audit retention limits.

7. No License or Warranty

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, patent, copyright, or trademark right by implication or otherwise. All Confidential Information is provided “AS IS” without warranty of any kind.

8. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any legal suit, action, or proceeding arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the federal or state courts situated in New York County, New York.
