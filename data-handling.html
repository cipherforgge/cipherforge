Operational Security // CF-DHP-03

Data Handling Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Lead Reviewer: Sarah Goldberg, Privacy & Compliance • Version 3.1
Policy Overview: This operational policy governs how Cipherforge engineers, forensic specialists, and contractors collect, process, store, and sanitize digital artifacts and client information throughout our research lifecycle. Direct inquiries to privacy@cipherforge.io.

1. Purpose and Foundational Principles

Cipherforge adheres to three foundational data governance principles:

  • Data Minimization: We extract and retain only the minimal technical evidence strictly required to substantiate a vulnerability, replicate an exploit, or determine root cause in an incident investigation.
  • Purpose Limitation: Information gathered during security assessments is utilized solely for technical verification and reporting back to the client entity.
  • Storage Limitation: Artifacts are governed by strict automated retention ceilings and cryptographic shredding routines.

2. Categories of Processed Data

Cipherforge categorizes engagement data into three security levels:

  • Tier 1 — Public / Commercial Contacts: Prospective client contact details, corporate communications, billing records.
  • Tier 2 — Engagement Telemetry & Logs: Network traffic traces, proxy logs, scanner telemetry, and system execution metadata.
  • Tier 3 — Sensitive Security Artifacts: Exploit proof-of-concept scripts, credentials discovered during audits, source code snippets, and customer database schema dumps.

3. Strict Retention Schedules

To eliminate lingering liability and protect client privacy, the following retention limits are strictly enforced:

  • Commercial & Contact Records: Retained for twenty-four (24) months following last contact for business continuity and invoicing records.
  • Project Findings & Audit Reports: Retained for twelve (12) months after delivery to facilitate remediation re-testing, unless client explicitly requests earlier deletion or legal compliance dictates otherwise.
  • Raw Network & Proxy Logs: Automatically purged after ninety (90) days via automated cryptographic sanitization routines.

4. Technical Access Controls and Defense

All client data resides within isolated security boundaries:

  • Encryption: AES-256 for all stored data; TLS 1.3 for all endpoints in transit. Encryption keys are rotated regularly and managed via dedicated HSMs.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Hardware-backed FIDO2 keys (e.g., YubiKey) are mandatory for all research infrastructure and repository access.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Researchers are provisioned access solely to the specific projects they are assigned to.
  • Quarterly Access Reviews: Access permissions and active cryptographic keys are reviewed and certified by our compliance team every calendar quarter.

5. Incident Management Protocol

Cipherforge operates an internal Security Incident Response Plan encompassing four phases:

  1. Triage: Immediate technical categorization within 60 minutes of anomaly detection.
  2. Containment: Isolation of affected virtual environments and revocation of active session tokens.
  3. Internal Escalation & Notification: Notification to executive leadership and impacted clients within 48 hours of confirmed impact.
  4. Post-Mortem & Lessons Learned: Complete root-cause remediation analysis and documentation updates.

6. Vendor Due Diligence & Secure Destruction

Every infrastructure partner and cloud provider utilized by Cipherforge is vetted against SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 standards.

Upon expiration of the retention periods, media sanitization is executed in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization) through cryptographic erasure and physical disk degaussing.
