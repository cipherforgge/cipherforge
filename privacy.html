Section 06 // Privacy Governance

Privacy policy

How we handle personal data.

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Controller: Cipherforge LLC (New York)
Privacy Commitment: Cipherforge is a cybersecurity consultancy built on trust. We do not sell personal data, do not run third-party advertising tracking scripts, and operate our digital infrastructure under strict data minimization standards. Direct privacy inquiries or rights requests to privacy@cipherforge.io.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Cipherforge or engage our research team, we may collect:

  • Inquiry & Communications Data: Full name, professional corporate email, company name, phone number, and message contents submitted via our contact channels.
  • Technical & System Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser user-agent, operating system version, referring URL, and basic security audit logs necessary to safeguard against denial-of-service and brute force attacks.
  • Engagement Artifacts: Staging and production configurations, API tokens, and technical documentation explicitly provided by client organizations during authorized security audits.

2. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal and technical data under the following legal bases pursuant to GDPR Article 6 and applicable US state privacy laws:

  • Performance of a Contract: To negotiate engagement scopes, execute penetration testing audits, and deliver findings reports.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our technical infrastructure against unauthorized probing, investigate security anomalies, and improve our services.
  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit an inquiry through our contact form requesting direct communication.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: To adhere to applicable statutory tax, accounting, and regulatory directives.

3. Minimal Cookie and Tracking Policy

Cipherforge maintains a privacy-first web presence:

  • We do not use third-party marketing, retargeting, or advertising pixels (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Pixel).
  • Only essential technical cookies or session tokens required for basic security and load-balancing may be utilized.

4. Information Sharing and Third Parties

Cipherforge will never monetize, lease, or distribute your personal data. We disclose data solely to:

  • Subprocessors: Trusted infrastructure providers providing secure hosting, DNS, and encrypted mail services bound by strict confidentiality terms.
  • Law Enforcement & Regulators: Only when strictly mandated by a legally binding court order or valid subpoena issued by a court of competent jurisdiction.

5. Retention Ceilings

We do not retain information longer than necessary:

  • Contact and inquiry logs: 24 months from last communication.
  • Audit technical findings: 12 months following report delivery.
  • System and connection logs: 90 days, purged via automated schedule.

6. Your Statutory Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your physical location and jurisdiction, you possess the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether Cipherforge processes your personal data and obtain a copy (Right of Access).
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request permanent erasure of your personal data (Right to be Forgotten).
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of any prospective commercial communication without retaliation.

To exercise any statutory right, email our Privacy Officer directly at privacy@cipherforge.io. We respond to all verified inquiries within 30 days.
