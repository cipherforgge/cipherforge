Section 01 // Overview

About us

Who we are and how we got here.

Cipherforge was founded in Manhattan by elite security researchers and reverse engineers with a singular premise: automated vulnerability scanners and generic compliance tick-boxes cannot defend against determined, human adversaries.

Our firm was built from the ground up by veterans of global responsible disclosure programs, financial red teams, and defensive infrastructure engineering. We bridge the gap between aggressive offensive exploration and rigorous corporate risk mitigation. We do not run automated scripts and call it an audit; we reverse engineer application logic, probe architectural edge cases, and simulate targeted attacks using current threat tradecraft.

Our core focus spans high-stakes industries where failure is catastrophic: tier-one commercial banking, regulated healthcare platforms, defense-adjacent infrastructure, and high-growth technology unicorns. Every engagement is strictly confidential, conducted under mutual non-disclosure, and focused on delivering reproducible proof-of-concept findings paired with actionable technical remediation.
