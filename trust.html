Our Institutional Commitment

Offensive cybersecurity research requires access to an organization’s most critical assets: proprietary codebase architectures, production APIs, cloud infrastructure, and sensitive internal logs.

Cipherforge operates under rigorous fiduciary and legal safeguards. We enforce mutual non-disclosure protections, strict data-at-rest encryption standards, zero-knowledge research enclaves, and defined data destruction protocols under New York State law.

Our governance framework ensures that findings, vulnerabilities, and artifacts uncovered during research remain exclusively confidential to authorized client stakeholders.
